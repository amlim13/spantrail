How it works

  1. Install the SDKFive lines to initialize. It reads SPANTRAIL_API_KEY and SPANTRAIL_ENDPOINT from the environment, so nothing is hardcoded.
  2. Wrap your workDecorate a function or open a span around a block. Everything else keeps working unchanged - the SDK swallows its own failures.
  3. Read the timelineEvents are batched in-process and flushed every few seconds over HTTPS to your own endpoint.
