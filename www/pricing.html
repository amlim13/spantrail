Questions

What counts as an event?

One span - a single step, tool call or model call in your agent. A trace with 20 spans is 20 events. Logs and traces are not counted.

What happens if I exceed my plan?

Ingest keeps working. The dashboard shows your usage against the limit so you can upgrade before it matters. We do not hard-stop your agents mid-run.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. The billing portal, reachable from your dashboard, lets you move between plans or cancel immediately. Cancel takes effect at the end of the paid period.

Do you charge for failed requests?

No. Only successful ingest calls are billed, by event count.

Where is my data?

In our Postgres instance on a single Hetzner box in your region. 30-day retention on Free and Pro, 90 days on Team.
